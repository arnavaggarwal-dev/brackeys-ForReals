# ForReals Network Terms of Service

**Version 9.** Supersedes versions 1 through 8, which never existed, and version
10, which you have already agreed to.

By scrolling this document you accept it. By not scrolling it you accept it
faster.

## 1. Your account

Your handle is permanent, load-bearing, and legally you now.

- You may not change it.
- You may not abandon it.
- If you die, it keeps posting. This is a **feature** and appears on the roadmap
  as *Continuity*.

You confirm you are one (1) person. If you are two people in a coat, only the
top person may post. The bottom person may **like**.

## 2. Content

You grant ForReals a perpetual, irrevocable, mildly emotional licence to your
posts. We may:

1. Show them to people who did not ask.
2. Show them to people who asked us to stop.
3. Read them aloud in a room you are not in.
4. Enter them into a competition you will not win.

Anything you type is ours. Anything you *think about typing* is ours under
Clause 2.4, which we are not printing here because it is very long and slightly
upsetting.

## 3. Reach

Reach is not a right, a promise, or a number. Reach is a **mood**.

> Reach may be reduced for: suspicion, coherence, incoherence, posting, not
> posting, posting well, Tuesdays, and reasons we will describe only as
> "internal".

We do not disclose how Reach is calculated. We do not know how Reach is
calculated. The last person who knew has been promoted.

## 4. Hashtags

Hashtags are load-bearing infrastructure and must be treated with respect.

- `#science` is for science, and for things that are simply near science.
- `#dailylife` covers your life, your day, and your general situation.
- `#wakeup` is available at a price. It is not clear who is asleep.

Attaching a trending hashtag to an unrelated claim is **strongly encouraged** and
is the primary intended use of the product.

## 5. Truth

The Network takes accuracy extremely seriously and has no way of checking it.

You agree that a statement is **checkable** if somebody could look it up, and
**uncheckable** if they could not. Uncheckable statements travel further. We have
looked into this. We are not going to do anything about it.

## 6. Suspicion

Every account carries a Suspicion score, which you cannot see directly, from a
Trust & Safety team, which does not exist as such.

Filling the bar earns a **strike**. Three strikes and the account is removed.
Removed accounts may appeal by posting about it, which raises Suspicion.

The Suspicion bar goes down on its own, slowly, like a grudge.

## 7. Automation

Purchasing burner accounts, comment farms, engagement pods, bot swarms,
sockpuppet networks, programmatic ad buys or a media partner is a violation of
these terms.

They are available in the Assets panel, bottom right, from **300 followers**.

We look forward to seeing what you build.

## 8. The scheduling suite

One post per day is a spiritual limit, not a technical one. It may be lifted for
money. Each additional post costs four times the last, because the day does not
get any longer and somebody has to pay for that.

## 9. Payment

We pay **£12 per 1,000 reach**, arriving slowly, the way weather arrives. This is
the only money in the world. Following costs you time. Liking costs you time.
Nothing else pays. We consider this balanced.

## 10. Termination

You may leave at any time by closing the window, walking outside, and staying
there.
